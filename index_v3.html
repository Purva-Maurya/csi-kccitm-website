01 — About

A community for
students who build.

Bridging academic learning with real-world technology through practical experiences, collaboration and professional growth.

Technology is not just something to learn — it is something to build, explore and use to create impact.

At CSI KCC ITM, we bring together students who want to explore technology beyond the classroom. Our chapter creates a space for students to develop practical skills, solve real-world problems and learn alongside like-minded peers.

Through technical workshops, hands-on sessions, expert talks, competitions, hackathons, projects, industry interactions and collaborative initiatives, we help students turn learning into action.
