# CSI KCC ITM Student Chapter

### Computer Society of India — KCC ITM Student Chapter

A student-led technical community at **KCC Institute of Technology & Management, Greater Noida**, dedicated to creating opportunities for students to **learn, build, innovate and connect**.

🌐 **Live Website:** [csi-kccitm.netlify.app](csi-kccitm2026b.netlify.app)

---

## About

The **CSI KCC ITM Student Chapter** aims to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world technology through practical learning, collaboration and professional development.

The chapter provides students with opportunities to explore emerging technologies, develop practical skills, work on projects, participate in competitions and hackathons, interact with industry professionals, and grow alongside like-minded peers.

> **Technology is not just something to learn — it is something to build, explore and use to create impact.**
